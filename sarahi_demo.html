How repayment is verified: nobody confirms it by hand. The repay_loan instruction moves the exact amount due from the borrower’s token account to the pool vault. If that transfer fails, the whole transaction reverts and no status changes. If it succeeds, the same transaction sets the Loan to Repaid and the Property to Released. Default works the other way: once the term ends, anyone can call mark_default and the program checks the clock. Releasing the deed from the legal trust off-chain, synced through the legal oracle, is on the roadmap.

If the loan is not repaid: 7-day auction

Days 1 to 3: TLC holders
Days 4 to 5: pool lenders
Days 6 to 7: public

Lenders are covered because the property is worth twice the loan. Priority goes to long-term TLC holders to discourage whales from sniping discounted homes.
